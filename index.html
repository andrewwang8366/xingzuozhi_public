Constellation Atlas · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Data collection

Constellation Atlas runs entirely offline. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not collect or upload any personal information. Star charts, zodiac glyphs and the animated night sky are all drawn locally on your device, and the star data ships inside the app.

Local storage

The app stores only the interface preferences it needs, on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions — no location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or network access.

Third parties

There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

About the content

Constellation dates, star names, magnitudes and deep-sky objects are public astronomical facts; the mythology and annotations were written for this app. The app is offered for cultural interest and enjoyment only. It contains no horoscopes, fortune telling or predictions of any kind, and the personality traits and famous names it lists are not predictions or advice.
